Success can kill a startup if the infrastructure isn't ready. We've helped SaaS companies scale from their first 100 users to millions of requests per day.

## The Scaling Checklist

*   **Database optimization**: Indexing strategies and read replicas are non-negotiable.
*   **Caching Layers**: Redis is your best friend. Cache aggressively.
*   **Serverless vs. Containers**: Knowing when to use Lambda functions vs. Kubernetes clusters to balance cost and control.

Building for scale doesn't mean over-engineering early, but it means making architectural choices that don't paint you into a corner later.
