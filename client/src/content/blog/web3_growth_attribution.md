 attribution in Web3 is broken.
Most marketers track "clicks" and then lose visibility the moment the user opens Metamask.
This leads to immense wasted spend. You might be optimizing for clicks, but paying for bots.

## The On-Chain Conversion Pixel
The solution is "Wallet-Aware" attribution.
By using tools like Spindl, Helika, or custom subgraphs, we can link an off-chain identifier (UTM param) to an on-chain address.
*   **Step 1**: User clicks ad (UTM captured).
*   **Step 2**: User connects wallet (Signature request links Cookie ID + Wallet Address).
*   **Step 3**: User buys NFT (On-chain event).
*   **Step 4**: Event is pushed back to Meta/Google as a "Purchase" conversion.

## Why This Changes Everything
Once you feed on-chain data back into the ad platform, the algorithm wakes up.
Instead of finding people who "click ads," it finds people who "hold >1 ETH and trade NFTs."
Your ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) doesn't just improve; it multiplies.

## Conclusion
Stop marketing blindly.
Treat the blockchain as your CRM.
