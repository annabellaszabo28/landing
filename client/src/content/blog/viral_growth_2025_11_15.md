User-generated content solves two problems simultaneously: it provides content at zero production cost and it creates engagement loops that retain creators. Our November 2025 research explores strategies for catalyzing and leveraging UGC.


## The UGC Flywheel

Users create content. Content attracts new users. New users become creators. The flywheel accelerates as each cycle adds both consumers and creators to the ecosystem.

According to [Stackla research](https://stackla.com/), user-generated content is perceived as 2.4x more authentic than brand-created content. This authenticity drives engagement and conversion.


## Lowering Creation Barriers

The lower the barrier to creation, the more creators participate. TikTok's success came partly from making video creation remarkably easy compared to YouTube.

Provide templates, tools, and prompts that enable creation. Remove technical barriers that prevent willing creators from participating.


## Motivation Design

People create for multiple reasons: self-expression, social validation, utility, and sometimes financial reward. Different motivations require different design approaches.

Leaderboards and metrics serve competition-motivated creators. Privacy controls serve expression-motivated creators. Monetization serves financially-motivated creators.

Understand which creator motivations your product can serve and design for them specifically.


## Quality Control Without Demotivation

Not all UGC is good. Some requires moderation. But heavy-handed moderation demotivates creators.

Community moderation scales better than centralized moderation. Clear guidelines prevent most problems. Algorithmic distribution can downrank low-quality content without removal.
