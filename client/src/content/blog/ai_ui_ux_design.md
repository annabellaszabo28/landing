The role of the UI/UX designer has shifted dramatically over the past year. With tools that can generate layout variations in seconds, the focus has moved from pixel-pushing to empathy and strategy.

## Generative Design in Practice

We use AI to:
*   **Generate Content**: Lorem Ipsum is dead. Real context leads to better design decisions.
*   **Rapid Prototyping**: Testing 10 variations of a landing page hero section in the time it used to take to design one.
*   **User Testing**: Simulating user flows with AI agents to identify bottlenecks before human testing.

This efficiency allows us to spend more time on what matters: crafting a unique brand identity and emotional connection.
