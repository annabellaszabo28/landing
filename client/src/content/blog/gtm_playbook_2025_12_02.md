The handoff from marketing to sales is where most B2B companies lose deals. Marketing generates leads, throws them over the wall, and wonders why sales cannot close. Sales blames lead quality. Marketing blames sales execution.

The real problem is context loss. Our December 2025 playbook focuses on maintaining buyer context through the entire journey.


## The Information Continuity Problem

By the time a lead reaches sales, they have consumed multiple pieces of content, visited multiple pages, and formed specific impressions. They expect the sales conversation to acknowledge this history.

When a sales rep starts from scratch, asking questions the buyer already answered through their behavior, trust erodes. The buyer feels like a number, not a known entity.

CRM integration that surfaces behavioral context is table stakes. Sales reps should know what content the prospect consumed before the first conversation begins.


## Buyer Journey Stage Identification

Not all leads are at the same stage. Some are researching the problem. Some are evaluating solutions. Some are ready to purchase but need internal justification support.

According to [Gartner research](https://www.gartner.com/), B2B buyers spend only seventeen percent of their journey talking to vendors. The rest is independent research and internal discussion.

Sales enablement means providing content for every stage: problem education for early stage, solution comparison for mid stage, ROI calculators and implementation guides for late stage.


## Objection Handling as Content Strategy

Every objection a sales rep hears represents a content gap. If prospects consistently ask about integration difficulty, create content addressing integration in detail. If they worry about implementation time, create case studies with timeline details.

Convert objection patterns into proactive content that handles concerns before they become blocking issues.
