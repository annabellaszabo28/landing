Most "viral" campaigns fail because they rely on sharing for the sake of sharing.
"Please share this with your friends!"
Why? No one cares.
True virality requires **Incentive Alignment**.

## The Double-Sided Reward
The gold standard is the Dropbox model: "You get 500MB, your friend gets 500MB."
It makes the sender look like a hero, not a spammer.
In Web3, we can supercharge this.
"You get 10 Tokens, your friend gets a Free NFT Mint."

## The K-Factor
The metric that matters is the **K-Factor**.
K = (Invites sent per user) * (Conversion rate of invites).
If K > 1, you have exponential growth. If K < 1, you have linear decay.
Our job is to push K above 1.
*   **Friction Reduction**: One-click sharing links (pre-filled text).
*   **Social Proof**: "3 of your friends are already here."

## Gamification
Leaderboards work.
People are competitive status monkeys.
"Top 10 referrers this week get a whitelist spot."
This drives "Super-Connectors" to go out and manually recruit for you.

## Conclusion
Hope is not a strategy.
Don't pray for a viral tweet. Engineer a viral loop.
