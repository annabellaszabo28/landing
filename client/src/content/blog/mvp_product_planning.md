The biggest mistake founders make is building before planning. In our product studio, we've seen countless ideas fail not because of bad code, but because of poor product strategy.

## The 4-Step Framework

1.  **Problem Validation**: Talk to 50 potential users before writing a line of code.
2.  **Solution Design**: Wireframe the core flow. If it doesn't make sense on paper, it won't make sense in code.
3.  **MVP Definition**: What is the absolute minimum feature set that delivers value? Cut everything else.
4.  **Feedback Loop**: Build, measure, learn. Repeat.

Successful product building is an iterative process. It requires discipline to say "no" to features that don't serve the immediate goal.
