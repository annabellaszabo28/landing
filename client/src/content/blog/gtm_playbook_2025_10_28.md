The graveyard of failed products is filled with solutions searching for problems. Our October 2025 GTM analysis confirms that eighty four percent of startup failures trace back to building something the market did not actually want.

The tragedy is that this failure mode is entirely preventable. Pre-launch validation is not expensive. Skipping it is expensive.

This playbook provides a practical framework for pressure-testing your value proposition before you commit significant resources to building.


## The Smoke Test Framework

Before you build anything, test whether anyone will click the buy button. Create a landing page that describes your product as if it already exists. Drive traffic through paid channels. Measure click-through on the purchase CTA.

You are not deceiving anyone. The page clearly states the product is coming soon and collects email signups. But users who click buy are demonstrating purchase intent, not just curiosity.

A smoke test that converts at one percent or higher indicates viable demand. Below that threshold, revisit your positioning or value proposition.


## Concierge MVP Validation

Before you build automation, deliver your value manually. According to advice from [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/), The concierge MVP tests whether customers will pay for the outcome, independent of how that outcome is achieved.

If you are building a matching algorithm, match people manually first. If you are building a data analysis tool, analyze the data in spreadsheets first. Validate the value before investing in the mechanism.


## The Mom Test for Customer Discovery

When you ask potential customers if they would buy your product, they will lie to be polite. The solution is to never ask about your product at all.

Instead, ask about their current behavior, their current problems, their current spending. If they are already paying to solve this problem badly, there is budget for solving it well. If they are not paying anything, they probably will not start.
