For a decade, "Mobile First" was the mantra of web design. But in late 2025, user behavior data tells a different story.

## The Engagement Gap

Users spend 7x more time in native apps than on mobile web. The friction of opening a browser, typing a URL, and dealing with non-native UI controls is becoming a barrier to conversion.

### Why You Need an App Strategy:

*   **Push Notifications**: Direct line of communication to your customers.
*   **Offline Functionality**: Keep users engaged even without connectivity.
*   **Better Performance**: Smoother animations and faster load times.

Whether it's a cross-platform solution or a fully native iOS/Android build, having an app is now a baseline expectation for serious brands.
