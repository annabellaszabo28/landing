Knowing which sources AI models currently cite for your target queries is essential for GEO strategy. You cannot displace what you do not understand. Our December 2025 research provides frameworks for competitive intelligence in the AI discovery landscape.


## Citation Monitoring Methodology

Systematic monitoring requires asking target queries across multiple AI platforms and documenting which sources get cited. This creates a competitive landscape map showing who owns which query territories.

The process is manual but essential. No tool currently provides reliable citation tracking across platforms. This creates opportunity for teams willing to invest effort.


## Pattern Recognition

After monitoring hundreds of queries, patterns emerge. Certain sources dominate certain topic areas. Certain content formats get cited more frequently. Certain positioning strategies correlate with citation success.

These patterns inform your content strategy. If competitors with FAQ formats get cited more than competitors with essay formats, adopt FAQ formats.


## Gap Identification

Competitive analysis reveals gaps: queries where no source gets cited, where citations are inconsistent, or where multiple weak sources compete. These gaps represent opportunity.

Dominating a gap is easier than displacing an established source. Prioritize gap opportunities before attacking defended positions.


## Displacement Strategy

When you must displace an established source, you need a superior asset: more comprehensive, more recent, more authoritative, or better structured. Marginal improvements rarely trigger displacement.

Analyze why the current leader wins. Identify specific dimensions where you can create clear superiority. Build assets that win on those dimensions.
