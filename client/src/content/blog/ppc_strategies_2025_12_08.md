Retargeting reaches users who have already demonstrated interest. These warm audiences convert at multiples of cold traffic rates. Our December 2025 research provides strategies for maximizing retargeting efficiency.


## The Recency Principle

User intent decays over time. Someone who visited your site today is more valuable than someone who visited a month ago. Recency segmentation is the foundation of retargeting efficiency.

According to [AdRoll research](https://www.adroll.com/), conversion rates for retargeted users decline approximately 15% per week after initial visit. Recency-based bid adjustments capture this decay.


## Journey Stage Segmentation

Not all website visitors are equal. Someone who viewed a product is warmer than someone who only saw the homepage. Someone who abandoned cart is warmest of all.

Segment retargeting audiences by funnel depth. Match messages to journey stage. Cart abandoners get urgency messaging. Content readers get education messaging.


## Frequency Capping Necessity

Retargeting without frequency caps creates annoyance. Users who see your ad twenty times start developing negative brand associations. The line between reminder and stalking is thin.

Set frequency caps that balance persistence with respect. Three to five impressions per day is typically the maximum before diminishing returns become negative returns.


## Cross-Platform Retargeting

Users see your retargeting ads across multiple platforms. Coordinate frequency and messaging across platforms to avoid overwhelming individual users.

Unified frequency management prevents the scenario where a user sees your ad five times on Facebook, five times on Google Display, and five times on LinkedIn in a single day.
