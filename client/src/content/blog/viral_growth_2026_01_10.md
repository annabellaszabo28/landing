Growth is a discipline requiring dedicated capacity. Without organizational investment, growth happens accidentally. Our January 2026 research explores how to structure teams for systematic growth.


## The Growth Function Definition

Growth sits at the intersection of product, marketing, engineering, and data. It requires cross-functional skills and organizational authority to run experiments across boundaries.

According to [Growth Hackers research](https://growthhackers.com/), companies with dedicated growth functions grow 19% faster than those without. The organizational investment pays for itself.


## Team Structure Options

Centralized growth teams maintain expertise and learning but may lack context for specific product areas. Embedded growth people maintain context but may lack coordination and learning sharing.

Hybrid models with a central growth team that embeds in product teams often work best. The central team maintains methodology while embedded members have product context.


## The Skills Mix

Growth teams need diverse skills: data analysis to identify opportunities, product sense to design solutions, engineering to implement experiments, and marketing to understand customer psychology.

Few individuals have all skills. Build teams that collectively cover the required capabilities.


## Organizational Authority

Growth teams without authority to run experiments cannot function. If engineering resources are entirely controlled by product roadmaps, growth cannot test independently.

Allocate reserved capacity for growth experimentation. Establish decision rights for experiment prioritization. Without authority, growth is just research.
