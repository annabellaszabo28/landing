Performance Max and similar AI-powered campaign types flip the traditional advertising model. Instead of manually selecting keywords, audiences, and placements, you provide assets and goals. The algorithm handles distribution.

Our January 2026 research explores how to succeed within this new paradigm.


## The Asset Quality Imperative

When algorithms choose distribution, your competitive differentiator becomes asset quality. The algorithm will test your assets against competitors' assets across all placements.

Invest heavily in creative: high-quality images, multiple video formats, varied headlines and descriptions. More assets give algorithms more optimization options.


## Goal Signal Clarity

Algorithms optimize toward goals you set. Unclear or conflicting goals create confused optimization. Be specific about what you actually want: leads, purchases, revenue, profit.

According to [Google's Performance Max documentation](https://support.google.com/google-ads/), campaigns with clear conversion values outperform those with simple conversion counting by 40%.


## The Black Box Challenge

AI campaigns provide less visibility into what is working. You know spend and results but may not know which placements, keywords, or audiences drove performance.

Accept partial visibility. Focus on inputs you can control: assets, goals, exclusions. Trust the algorithm with distribution while maintaining overall performance accountability.


## Exclusion Management

You cannot tell Performance Max where to go, but you can tell it where not to go. Exclusions for brand safety, competitor content, and irrelevant placements maintain quality.

Review placement reports regularly. Add exclusions for locations that consistently underperform or misalign with brand values.
