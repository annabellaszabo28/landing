## 1. The Core Components of GEO

Unlike traditional SEO, which focused on vanity metrics like backlinks and dwell time, GEO is centered on machine readable fact clusters and data provenance.


### What AI Engines Look For

Quotability: Content must contain clear, concise definitions and unique data points that are easy for an LLM to cite.

Structural Rigor: Using tables, lists, and [semantic HTML](/blog/ai_agent_behavior_analysis) makes it significantly easier for AI agents to parse your site.

Expert Analysis: AI favors information that provides unique human insights rather than commodity software patterns.


## 2. Optimizing for Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)

RAG is the process by which AI models retrieve relevant knowledge chunks from the web before generating a response.

To win the citation game, your content must be selected as one of these truth fragments.


### Strategy for RAG Optimization

Knowledge Fragmentation: Break down complex whitepapers into descriptive, modular sections.

Primary Source Data: Publish proprietary research and technical benchmarks that others (including AIs) must reference.

Schema Gating: Rigorously apply schema markup to define your relationship to industry [pillars](/marketing/strategy) as recommended by [Schema.org](https://schema.org/).


## 3. Brand Entity Optimization (BEO)

AI models trust sources that other trusted sources cite.

In 2026, the quantity of your backlinks is irrelevant.
