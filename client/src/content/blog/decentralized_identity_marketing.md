Advertising used to be easy. You put a pixel on a user's browser and followed them around the internet.
GDPR, CCPA, and Apple's ATT have destroyed that model.
The future of marketing data isn't *taking* it from users. It's *asking* for it.
And the mechanism for that is **Decentralized Identity (DID)**.

## What is a DID?
A DID is a user-owned profile. It's a digital backpack containing their verified credentials (KYC status, accreditation), their on-chain history (NFTs owned, protocols used), and their reputation.
Crucially, **the user controls the key**.

## Marketing in a DID World
How do you target ads when you can't track cookies?
You target **credentials**.
*   **Permissioned Marketing**: You offer a user a reward (tokens, access) in exchange for "reading" their DID.
*   **Soulbound Tokens (SBTs)**: You issue non-transferable tokens to your top users. These act as on-chain loyalty cards. "Holders of the 'Early Adopter' SBT get 20% off forever."

## Zero-Knowledge Compliance
The killer app for enterprise is **Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZK)**.
You can verify a user is "Over 18" or "Accredited Investor" *without* ever seeing their passport or name.
This reduces your liability to zero.

## Conclusion
Data privacy is not a hurdle; it's a feature.
By embracing DID and [Web3 Tools](/web3), you build a relationship based on consent, not surveillance.
