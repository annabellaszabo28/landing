Creative fatigue is the primary reason PPC campaigns fail.

Even the most successful ad eventually dies as the audience becomes blind to the visuals.

In the legacy world, a campaign would see an increasing cost per action until a manager noticed.

In our AI native system, we implement perpetual optimization.

The AI monitors the cost per action and the click through rate at the minute level.

The moment a winning creative dips below its average performance, the system acts.

It automatically rotates in the next challenger variation from the library.

The system learns from the failure to improve the next batch of assets.

If minimalist ads start to fatigue, the system directs the generator to produce high contrast assets.

Long decision cycles require consistent re engagement with fresh visuals.
