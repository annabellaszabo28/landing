In 2024, a good media buyer tested 5 new ads a week.
In 2026, our [Predictive Performance](/marketing) engine tests 500.
The platform algorithms (Meta, TikTok, Google) have become black boxes. You can't out-hack the targeting. Broad targeting works best.
So the *only* lever left is **Creative**.

## The Creative Testing Factory
We don't rely on human intuition to guess what image will work. We use AI to generate variations.
*   **Hook Testing**: We generate 50 text hooks ("Stop losing money," "Start earning yield," "The secret to...").
*   **Visual Testing**: We swap backgrounds, colors, and art styles instantly using GenAI.
*   **Format Testing**: We convert static images into short-form videos automatically.

## Buying the "Winner"
Once the algorithm finds a winner (High CTR, Low CPA), we double down.
But we know "ad fatigue" sets in fast.
So the moment performance dips by 10%, the AI automatically rotates in the next best variation.
This is **Perpetual Optimization**.

## Data-Driven Feedback Loop
The results from the ads feed back into the product.
If ads mentioning "Security" get the highest clicks, we tell the [Product Planning](/services/product-planning) team to highlight security features in the app.
Marketing informs Product, not just the other way around.

## Conclusion
Don't fall in love with your creative.
Let the data decide what is beautiful.
