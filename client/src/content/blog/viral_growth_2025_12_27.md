Growth without retention is a leaky bucket. You can pour in users but they leak out faster than you add them. Our December 2025 research explores why retention is the prerequisite for sustainable growth.


## The Retention Math

If you retain 80% of users monthly, after 12 months you have 7% remaining. If you retain 95%, you have 54% remaining. Small retention differences compound into massive cohort differences.

According to growth economics, improving retention by 5% is often worth more than improving acquisition by 25%. The math strongly favors retention investment.


## Diagnosing Retention Problems

Retention problems have different causes requiring different solutions. Early churn indicates onboarding failure. Late churn indicates value exhaustion. Sudden cohort degradation indicates product or competitor changes.

Segment retention analysis by cohort, acquisition source, and onboarding completion to identify specific failure points.


## The Aha Moment Framework

Users who reach the aha moment, where they experience core value, retain dramatically better than those who do not. Identify your aha moment and design onboarding to reach it as fast as possible.

Different users may have different aha moments. Personalized onboarding that matches users to their relevant aha moment improves outcomes.


## Resurrection and Win-Back

Some churned users can be recovered. Win-back campaigns targeting users who churned recently often achieve reasonable response rates.

But resurrection investment should follow retention investment. Fix why users leave before trying to bring back those who already left.
