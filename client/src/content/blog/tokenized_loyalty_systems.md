Traditional loyalty programs are walled gardens.
Your airline miles are stuck with the airline. Your coffee points are stuck in the app.
This creates "Liability" on the company balance sheet and "Frustration" for the user.
Web3 flips this model.

## Liquidity = Value
When you tokenize a loyalty point (fungible) or a status tier (NFT), you make it tradable.
Suddenly, your points have a market price.
*   **Secondary Market Royalties**: If a user sells their "Gold Status" NFT, the brand collects a 5% royalty. You monetize churn.
*   **Interoperability**: A hotel chain can accept "Airline Tokens" without a complex B2B partnership; they just integrate the token standard.

## Case Study: Starbucks Odyssey
Starbucks didn't call them "NFTs." They called them "Stamps."
But the result was the same. Users began *collecting* brand assets, trading them, and engaging deeper than ever before.
The asset ownership created a psychological lock-in stronger than any punch card.

## Conclusion
Don't build a prison for your customers.
Build an economy.
