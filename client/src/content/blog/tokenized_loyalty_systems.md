Traditional loyalty programs function as walled gardens.

Your airline miles stay with the airline.

Your coffee points reside only in the app.

This model creates a liability on the corporate balance sheet.

It creates frustration for the user.

Points often expire before a user can spend them.

In 2026, Web3 technology flipped this model.

Tokens replaced points.

Assets replaced liabilities.

BlockMarketing Consulting lead the transition to tokenized retention.

Liquidity represents the core value of the new model.

When you tokenize a loyalty point, you make it tradable.

Status tiers become liquid assets.

A market price emerges for brand loyalty.

Secondary market royalties change the game for businesses.

If a user sells their gold status NFT, the brand collects a royalty.

The brand monetizes user churn.

A departing customer provides one last stream of revenue.

Interoperability allows for cross brand loyalty without friction.

A hotel chain can accept airline tokens without a complex partnership.

They simply integrate the token standard on the ledger.

Users enjoy a seamless experience across multiple brands.

Case studies provide proof of this success.

Starbucks Odyssey used stamps instead of the term NFT.

The result was the same.

Users began collecting brand assets.

They started trading their loyalty.

Engagement reached levels higher than legacy programs.

Asset ownership creates a psychological lock in.

It is stronger than any punch card.

The user feels like a stakeholder.

They own a piece of the brand's digital presence.
