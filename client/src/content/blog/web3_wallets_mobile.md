The barrier to entry for Web3 is still too high. Mobile apps are the key to mass adoption, but wallet integration has historically been clunky.

## The New Standard: Account Abstraction

With ERC-4337 and modern embedded wallets, we can now create "invisible" web3 experiences.

*   **Social Login**: Users sign in with Google or Apple, a wallet is created in the background.
*   **Gasless Transactions**: Pay the gas fees for your users to remove friction.
*   **Biometric Signing**: Use FaceID to sign transactions instead of complex seed phrases.

We specialize in building these hybrid Web2/Web3 mobile experiences that feel like magic, not finance.
