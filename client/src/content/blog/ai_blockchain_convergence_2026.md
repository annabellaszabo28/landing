## The Role of Privacy Infrastructure: Arcium and Beyond

Privacy is the "missing link" for widespread AI-DeFi adoption. For an AI agent to manage a user's sensitive financial data or execute private business logic, it needs an environment where that data is processed securely—without being exposed on a public, transparent ledger.

Infrastructure projects like Arcium and Oasis Network are solving this through Confidential Computing and Multi-Party Computation (MPC). This allows agents to process "blind data," where the machine can execute its instructions without ever actually "seeing" the private keys or personal identifiers of the user.

Recent Ethereum Improvement Proposals (EIPs), such as EIP-7702 and the progress in Account Abstraction (as discussed in our [Onboarding Revolution guide](/blog/account_abstraction_growth)), are further catalyzing this convergence. These improvements create specialized trust layers designed specifically for autonomous agent interactions, allowing them to pay their own gas fees and recover their own social identities.
