Good design looks good. Great design feels right. The difference often lies in behavioral psychology.

## Cognitive Biases in UX

*   **Hick's Law**: The time it takes to make a decision increases with the number and complexity of choices. Simplify your UI.
*   **Peak-End Rule**: People judge an experience largely based on how they felt at its peak and at its end. Optimize your success states.
*   **Variable Reward**: The core of habit-forming products (like social media feeds).

We apply these principles to create products that users don't just use, but love.
