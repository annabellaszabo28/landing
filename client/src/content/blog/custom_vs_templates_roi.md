It's a common dilemma for business owners: buy a $60 component kit or invest thousands in custom development?

## The Hidden Cost of Templates

Templates are great for validating an idea. But they come with bloat, generic design, and limited functionality.

### When Custom Wins:

1.  **Unique Workflows**: If your business process is your competitive advantage, off-the-shelf software won't fit.
2.  **Performance**: Custom code only includes what you need, resulting in faster load times and better SEO.
3.  **Brand Authority**: A unique digital presence signals market leadership.

For our clients, the switch to custom applications typically yields a 40-60% increase in conversion rates within the first quarter.
