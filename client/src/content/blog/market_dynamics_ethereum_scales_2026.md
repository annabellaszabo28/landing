## Bitcoin's Dip and the Institutional Floor

Despite the price drop to $92K, spot ETFs saw $1.4B in net inflows this week—the strongest performance since October. This suggests a powerful "institutional floor" is forming.

The liquidations were largely driven by over-leveraged retail positions reacting to US-EU tariff concerns. Institutional players, however, appear to be buying the dip with conviction.
