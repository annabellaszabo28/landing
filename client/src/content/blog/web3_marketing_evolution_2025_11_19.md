Enterprise buyers have different concerns than crypto-native users. Security, integration, support, and vendor stability matter more than decentralization ideology. Our November 2025 research explores B2B positioning for Web3.


## Speaking Enterprise Language

Enterprise buyers do not care about blockchain. They care about outcomes: reduced costs, new capabilities, competitive advantage. Translate blockchain benefits into business outcomes.

According to enterprise sales research, technical buyers need technical validation. Business buyers need business justification. Address both audiences with appropriate messaging.


## The Integration Question

Enterprises cannot adopt solutions that do not integrate with existing infrastructure. Integration capability is a primary buying criterion that blockchain projects often underemphasize.

Document integration paths. Provide reference architectures. Offer professional services for implementation. Make adoption easy.


## Vendor Stability Concerns

Enterprises need confidence that vendors will exist and support products long-term. The volatility of Web3 creates legitimate concern about vendor stability.

Address stability directly: funding runway, team experience, customer references, and commitment to enterprise support. Remove the objection rather than ignoring it.


## Procurement Process Alignment

Enterprise purchases follow procurement processes with security reviews, legal reviews, and budget approvals. Products that fit procurement processes sell; products that fight them lose.

Prepare security documentation, acceptable terms, and compliance certifications that procurement requires. Reduce friction in the buying process.
