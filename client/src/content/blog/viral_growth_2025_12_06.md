Communities create advocacy at scale. When users feel genuine connection to your brand and each other, they become voluntary ambassadors. Our December 2025 research explores how to build communities that drive systematic growth.


## Beyond User Groups

True community is not a support forum or user group. It is genuine connection between members based on shared identity, values, or goals. The product is a facilitator, not the entire relationship.

According to community research, products with strong communities have 4x the retention of products without community. Community creates switching costs through relationships.


## The Belonging Mechanism

People join communities for belonging. They feel part of something. Design community experiences that create this feeling: shared language, inside jokes, recognition systems, and identity markers.

Strong communities have recognizable culture. Members can identify each other. Outsiders can recognize members.


## Champion Programs

Your most passionate users can be formalized into champion programs. These advocates get special access, early features, and recognition in exchange for organized advocacy.

Champion programs scale word of mouth by providing structure and motivation. Champions become your most authentic marketing channel.


## Organic vs Manufactured Community

Communities that feel manufactured repel members. Authenticity cannot be faked. But authentic community can be facilitated.

Provide spaces for connection. Seed with genuine enthusiasts. Let culture emerge rather than dictating it. Your role is gardener, not architect.
