Product-Led Growth (PLG) puts your software at the center of the buying journey. It's not just a buzzword; it's how the fastest-growing companies win.

## Engineering Viral Loops

A PLG strategy requires deep integration between marketing and product engineering.

*   **Self-Service Onboarding**: Can a user get to "Aha!" moment without talking to sales?
*   **Network Effects**: Does the product get better when users invite their team?
*   **Usage-Based Pricing**: Aligning cost with value derived.

We help companies transition from sales-led to product-led, unlocking lower CAC and higher LTV.
