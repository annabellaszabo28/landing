Platform algorithms have become sophisticated bidding partners. But handing control to automation without understanding its behavior leads to unpredictable results. Our December 2025 research explores how to work with bidding algorithms for optimal outcomes.


## The Manual Starting Point

New campaigns benefit from manual bidding initially. Manual bidding provides control and generates data about keyword, audience, and placement performance.

This data phase establishes baselines. Without baselines, you cannot evaluate whether automation improves performance or simply changes behavior in unknown ways.

According to [Google Ads best practices](https://support.google.com/google-ads/), campaigns need at least 30 conversions over 30 days before automated bidding strategies can optimize effectively.


## Automated Strategy Selection

Different automated strategies optimize for different goals. Target CPA optimizes for cost efficiency. Target ROAS optimizes for revenue efficiency. Maximize conversions optimizes for volume.

Match the strategy to your actual business goal. Using the wrong strategy means optimization toward the wrong objective.


## Learning Period Management

When you switch to automated bidding, algorithms enter learning periods where performance may be volatile. Avoid making additional changes during learning periods.

Patience is required. The learning period feels uncomfortable as performance fluctuates. Resist the urge to intervene until the algorithm stabilizes.


## The Human Oversight Layer

Automation optimizes within constraints you set. Setting wrong constraints means optimal execution of wrong strategy. Review automated campaigns regularly for alignment with business goals.

Watch for boundary cases: when algorithms spend heavily on unexpected segments or avoid valuable segments entirely.
